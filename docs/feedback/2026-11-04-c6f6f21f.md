## Deferred on purpose
These were left by the operator's rulings or the slice's verify sitting, and none is in `TODO.md`:
- **Voigt convolution centring:** the grid's centring offset (`isar/analyze/lineshapes.py:181`, `:484`) shifts a floor-width line by about one internal step; it was out of scope.
- **All-or-nothing covariance:** one near-zero mode with its width railed at a limit removes every mode's error bars in the refit's `_cov`. Today's behaviour, kept by the operator's ruling that each mode reports the fit's own error bars.
- **`width_at_limit` on a contradicted hinted mode:** it can fire on a link-added mode whose height went to zero. Watch for it on the 302 K batches.
- **Failed round-1 refit:** a simultaneous batch whose round-1 refit fails never gets the link's adds and splits, and nothing records that.
- **Separability weighting:** the closed-form separability weights counts by channel spacing, not exposure, so QC gaps and uneven exposure skew it. Noted in `docs/STATS_PRIMER.md` §2.5. The closed form also meets the original 25% acceptance band in only 8 of 16 synthetic cells, fading early for broad pairs; the operator chose to judge it on real data.
- **One-point spectrum:** a spectrum with a single kept point makes `np.gradient` raise when the engine is built. Not reachable in normal use.
- **Ring-mate energy coupling:** in an anchored ring, a mode the link adds from a ring-mate gets no energy factor to that ring-mate, because identity treats ring-mates as seams only. The operator chose to leave it ("(A) is fine"); the simultaneous batch that follows couples them.
