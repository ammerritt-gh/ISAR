*What good looks like:* the ω₀ rungs agree with each other near 0.68 (HESSE does not degrade on a location parameter); the Γ rows and the skewed-cell footnote show whether the symmetric Hessian band under-covers the bootstrap once the Γ posterior is right-skewed — measured on known truth, an honest result either way. But this is an ensemble average: matching coverage here does not rescue any single measurement (see the per-scan width-ratio figure) — the per-scan hesse_trusted / interval_reliable flags are what protect one scan.

*Terms:* **bootstrap** — resample the data many times and refit to get the spread of an estimate; **Hessian** — the curvature (HESSE) error bar — fast, but optimistic when the cost is not parabolic (Γ near its bound), honest when it is (ω₀); **at-bound** — Γ railed to its floor → the interval degenerates; excluded from the Γ coverage number and counted separately
