**Per-scan interval-width ratio — one scan is not the ensemble** — box plots of the per-scan width(HESSE)/width(bootstrap) ratio by count level: (a) ω₀ over all converged scans, (b) Γ over at-bound-excluded scans (with the skewed Γ_true=0.10 cell appended). The grey band is the [0.80, 1.25] agreement window (log-symmetric: a HESSE bar within ±25% of the bootstrap), the dashed line is exact agreement (ratio 1.0), and each box is annotated with the fraction of scans falling OUTSIDE the band and its N. Built from the committed coverage_perscan.json (median/IQR/frac-outside also in coverage_summary.json → diagnostics.width_ratio); seed 20260710.

*What good looks like:* the boxes straddle 1.0 with most scans inside the band — but the spread and the frac-outside are the point: coverage is an ensemble average, and a single scan can still draw a HESSE bar well off its bootstrap. That per-scan disagreement is exactly what the hesse_trusted / interval_reliable flags catch, scan by scan.

*Terms:* **bootstrap** — resample the data many times and refit to get the spread of an estimate; **width ratio** — one scan's HESSE interval width ÷ its bootstrap width; 1.0 = the fast bar matches the honest one, far from 1.0 = trust the bootstrap on THIS scan; **IQR** — inter-quartile range (25th–75th percentile) — the box height; how much the ratio varies scan to scan
