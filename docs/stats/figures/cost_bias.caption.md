*Terms:* **Baker–Cousins** — the Poisson −2lnL; its constant-rate minimizer is the arithmetic mean (unbiased); **modified-Pearson χ²** — Σ(y−f)²/f; minimizer is the RMS, biased high; **Neyman χ²** — Σ(y−f)²/max(y,1); minimizer is the harmonic mean, biased low
