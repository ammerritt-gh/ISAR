4 · The two confirmations: stopping a deep pass, and closing ISAR with work queued

Stopping a running fast analyzer loses about a minute and asks nothing. Stopping a running deep analyzer, and closing ISAR with anything running or waiting, ask first and name what is lost. Removing a waiting item asks nothing: it is re-added in one click.
