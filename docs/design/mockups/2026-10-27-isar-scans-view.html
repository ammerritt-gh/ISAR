The strip under the plot shares the energy axis and shows data − fit in counts. Offset | Overlay is greyed because only one spectrum is shown. This frame is Raw (y axis “Counts”); Normalised is the default (default, operator ruling).

2 · Five spectra, Offset, Deep

a03, a04, a05, a06 and a16 are selected with Ctrl-click. Chip 3 · 5 edited is the recalled group the operator then changed, so it carries “edited” (ctrl+3 would overwrite it). The traces are stacked in scan-list order (a03 on top). Data markers are neutral grey with a different symbol per spectrum; the fit curves are coloured by role and are the same colour on every trace, so Mode 1 can be followed up the stack. The legend has two parts: Spectra (symbol) and Curves (colour). a16 has no deep pass: under Deep there is no fallback, so its trace shows data only (no curves) and its legend line reads “a16 · 97 K · no deep pass”. The curves heading says “Deep (untrusted)”.
